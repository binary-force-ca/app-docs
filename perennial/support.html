Perennial: Habit Tracker helps you keep daily and weekly habits, with streaks, a completion rate and a calendar for each habit. Questions or a problem? Email binary.force.ca@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Using Perennial

How do I add a habit?

On Today, choose Daily or Weekly and tap New Habit. Give it a name and an emoji; for a weekly habit, pick the weekdays it is scheduled on.

How do I mark a habit done, or fill in a day I missed?

Tap the circle next to a habit on Today. To fill in an earlier day, open the habit and tap that day in its calendar.

What does "skip" do?

Long-press a day (a habit on Today, or a day in the calendar) to skip it, for a rest day or when you're ill. A skipped day keeps your streak going but doesn't add to it, and it is left out of your completion rate.

What does "archive" do?

Archiving pauses a habit: it leaves Today, and while it is archived nothing counts against it. Your streak and rate stay exactly as they were. Find it under Archived to restore it or delete it.

Your data

Where are my habits stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no Perennial server, so we can't see or restore your habits. Your device's own backup (iCloud, or Android's backup to your Google account) may include them.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habit from its detail screen, or delete the app to remove everything. The privacy policy has the details.

Ads and privacy

Why is there an ad?

Perennial is free thanks to one small banner at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. It never covers your habits.

Will Perennial ask to track me?

Perennial asks once, after you continue past a short note. Once you've used it on a few days, it shows that note, and then your iPhone asks. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Personalized ads.

I never saw Apple's question, and Perennial has no "Allow Tracking" switch in iOS Settings.

Check iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking: when Allow Apps to Request to Track is off, iOS never shows the question to any app, counts the answer as no, and shows no per-app switch.

How do I change Google's privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, open Settings on Today and tap Privacy choices. On Android, Settings, Personalized ads opens your phone's ad settings.
