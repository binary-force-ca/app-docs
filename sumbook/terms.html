Sumbook Pro and subscriptions

Sumbook Pro is optional. It adds extras (unlimited explained hints, the full daily archive, the Night theme and cage patterns, and a time trend in Stats) and removes ads. You can get it in three ways, and the price and period of each are shown in the app, in your local currency, before you buy:

  • Monthly - a subscription that renews every month.
  • Yearly - a subscription that renews every year.
  • Lifetime - a one-time purchase that does not renew.
  • Payment. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account (App Store) or your Google Play account at the time you confirm the purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Automatic renewal. A subscription renews automatically at the price and for the period shown unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time. On iPhone and iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or use the App Store's account page). On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. The app's Pro screen has a Manage subscription button that opens the right page. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • When a subscription ends. Pro turns off at the end of the period you paid for (the app notices the next time it checks with the store, for example when you open it or return to the Home screen). You keep everything you earned: your history, streaks, statistics, the days you unlocked, and your settings. Pro-only settings, such as the Night theme, fall back to their free equivalents and come back if you subscribe again.
  • Lifetime. Lifetime gives you Pro for as long as the app is offered, on the account you bought it with. Buying Lifetime does not cancel a subscription you already have: cancel that yourself in the store.
  • Restoring. If you reinstall the app or change device, tap Restore purchases (Pro screen or Settings) with the same Apple or Google account.
  • Refunds. Purchases are made through the store, so refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, Pro turns off.
  • Prices. Prices and taxes are set by the store and can change. A change to a subscription's price is announced by the store, and for existing subscribers it applies as the store allows.
  • Family Sharing. Sumbook Pro is not available through Family Sharing.
