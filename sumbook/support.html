Sumbook - Support

Sumbook: Killer Sudoku. A Daily puzzle, no ads during play. Need a hand? Start with the answers below, or email us.

How do I play?

A killer sudoku is a sudoku with no starting digits to speak of: instead, the grid is divided into cages, each showing a small sum. The digits in a cage must add up to its sum and never repeat. The app's Learn section walks you through it in five short lessons, and the Cage helper (the Σ button) lists every combination that fits a cage.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Puzzles, hints, the Daily puzzle and your stats all work offline. Ads and purchases need a connection only when you use them. There is no account.

My Daily streak was lost or paused

Streaks are protected against device-clock changes. If your clock is set back, the streak pauses until it is correct again. You get one streak freeze per week, and you can earn extra ones by watching an optional ad.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

The whole game is free: every difficulty, a new Daily every day with your streak, three explained hints a day, the cage helper, the Light theme in every color and the last 7 days of past Dailies. Sumbook Pro is optional. It adds unlimited explained hints, the full daily archive, the Night theme and cage patterns, and a time trend in Stats, and it removes ads.

What are the Pro options?

You can choose Monthly or Yearly (both renew automatically until you cancel) or Lifetime (paid once, never renews). The Pro screen shows the exact prices from the App Store or Google Play, and the Terms of Use explain renewal and cancelling.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (or use the same button on the Pro screen) with the same Apple or Google account you bought Pro with. Pro also comes back by itself when you open the app, if the store can be reached.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by the app, and deleting the app does not cancel one. Open the Pro screen and tap Manage subscription, or go to it yourself:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sumbook.
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sumbook.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Your history, streaks and the days you unlocked are never deleted; Pro-only settings such as the Night theme go back to their free versions and return if you subscribe again.

I bought Lifetime but I'm still being charged

Buying Lifetime does not cancel a subscription you already have. Cancel the subscription as described above; Lifetime keeps working.

Pro didn't turn on, or says purchases are unavailable

Make sure you are online and signed in to the App Store or Google Play, that purchases aren't turned off on the device (Screen Time on iPhone, parental controls on Android), then tap Restore purchases. If a purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy, or a slow payment method), Pro turns on as soon as it goes through. For refunds, contact Apple or Google: they handle billing. If it still doesn't work, email us with your device model and the app version.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows a full-screen ad now and then between puzzles (never during a puzzle, never after the Daily). Sumbook Pro removes them for as long as you have it.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy and the Terms of Use.
