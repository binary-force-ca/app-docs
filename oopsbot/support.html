Oops Bot - Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to suggest something? Email binary.force.ca@gmail.com. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your phone's model, its iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap any empty square to place your mark. Oops thinks for a moment, then places its own. Three of the same mark in a line, across, down or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up with no line, it's a draw. Tap New game to play again. Your score is under Oops's speech bubble.

Why is Oops so easy to beat?

That's the idea. From version 1.2.0 (on the Apple Watch too), Oops guesses most of its moves, on purpose, and doesn't pretend otherwise. It grabs a win when it sees one and blocks your winning square about half the time, and the rest of the time it picks a square at random. In earlier versions Oops picked a random empty square, on purpose too. It isn't broken: Oops Bot is meant to be a relaxed game you can usually win, with a robot that is a good sport about it. Oops still wins now and then, when its guesses happen to line up.

What does "Oops tries harder" do?

Open Settings with the gear in the top-right corner and turn on Oops tries harder. From version 1.2.0, Oops then takes its wins, blocks yours and goes for the middle and corners. It still slips up now and then, so you can still beat it. (In versions before 1.2.0 it grabs a win when it sees one and sometimes blocks yours, and the rest of the time it's still guessing.) Look for the sweatband on its forehead: Oops wears it in every game where it's trying harder. Changes start on a fresh board, never in the middle of a game. The Apple Watch app has no switch.

Can I play with a friend?

From version 1.2.0, on iPhone and Android phones (not on the Apple Watch). Open Settings with the gear, and under Play with choose A friend. You pass the phone back and forth: X is indigo and goes first, O is coral, and Oops stays on the screen as the host, announcing whose turn it is and keeping a score for the session. That score isn't saved: closing the app starts it again from zero, and it never goes into your own tally against Oops. Choose Oops to play against the robot again.

Can I play as O, or let Oops start?

Yes. In Settings, Play as picks X or O, and Who starts picks You, Oops, or Take turns. Your marks are always indigo and Oops's are coral, whichever letter you play, and X and O also differ by shape.

What does Vibration do?

With Vibration on in Settings, you feel a light tap when you place a mark and a buzz when a game ends. Turn it off and the app stops using haptics completely. If it's on and you feel nothing, check that haptics are turned on in your iPhone's Sounds & Haptics settings, or on Android in Settings › Sound & vibration › Vibration & haptics.

No sound?

From version 1.2.0, on iPhone and Android phones (not on the Apple Watch), Oops makes small sounds for your marks, its moves and the end of a game. If you hear nothing, check that Settings › Sound in the app isn't Off (it starts on Soft; Loud is louder). On iPhone the sounds follow the Ring/Silent switch on the side, so flip it to Ring, and turn the volume up. On Android the sounds follow your media volume: turn it up with a volume key while the game is on screen. The sounds mix with your music and never pause it.

How do I reset my score?

This version has no reset button. To start from zero, delete the app and install it again. That clears your score and your streaks, and also resets your settings.

Is my score backed up?

Not by us. Your score and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no account and no server, which means we cannot see them, and equally cannot recover them for you. Deleting the app deletes them. If your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud, or your Android phone backs up to your Google account, Oops Bot's data may be included in that backup, and a new phone set up from it may bring your score back.

Why is there an ad? Can I remove it?

Oops Bot is free and supported by one small banner at the bottom of the screen. From version 1.2.0, if you want a break from it, tap "Watch to hide ads" under the board (just above the banner, once it has loaded): one short video, and the banner stays hidden until you close the app. Closing the app brings it back, and closing the video early changes nothing. From 1.2.0 the banner can show ads that match your interests if you allow it (Apple's question on iPhone, Google's form in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland); otherwise it shows non-personalized ads. In versions before 1.2.0 the banner is the only ad, it stays on screen, and every ad request is child-directed, rated G and non-personalized. The Apple Watch app has no ads. The privacy policy explains the details.

I saw an ad that seems wrong. How do I report it?

Open Settings (the gear), scroll to About and tap Report an ad. Your mail app opens with a message to us. Tell us what the ad showed and when you saw it; a screenshot helps. If no mail app opens, write to binary.force.ca@gmail.com directly.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Only the banner needs a connection. Without one, the game plays exactly the same and the space at the bottom stays empty.

Does Oops Bot track me?

Versions before 1.2.0 (iPhone 1.0.0 and 1.1.0, Android 1.0.0) never ask about tracking and never track you. From version 1.2.0 it depends on your phone.

iPhone: only if you say so. After you have won a game or finished two, the next New game tap shows one Oops screen, and its Continue button leads to Apple's tracking question. Allow it and the banner can match your interests; choose Ask App Not to Track and it stays non-personalized. The app never asks again, and you can change your mind in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Android: there is no question in the app. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the banner can use Android's advertising ID to match your interests. You can reset that ID or turn off ad personalization in Android Settings, under Privacy, Ads (Settings, About, Personalized ads in the app leads there). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form decides.

The app has no account and no analytics of its own. See the privacy policy for what Google's ad SDK collects.

What is Privacy choices in Settings?

From version 1.2.0, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and in some US states) Settings, About shows a Privacy choices row. It opens Google's privacy options, where you can change your ad consent whenever you like. If you don't see it, your region doesn't need it.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Every square is labeled, for example "Row 1, column 2, empty", and Oops's moves and the result of each game are announced. When Reduce Motion is on, Oops's animations are turned off.

Does it work on Android?

Yes, on Android phones, with the same game, settings and score. A few things work the Android way:

  • The Back button or gesture closes Settings. On the game it sends the app to the background, and your game is still there when you come back.
  • TalkBack reads the same labels as VoiceOver, and Oops's moves and results are announced. When Remove animations is on (Settings › Accessibility), Oops's animations are turned off.
  • Changing the font size, display size, bold text or language while a game is in progress restarts that game. Your score and settings are kept.
  • The app asks for no runtime permission. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it uses Android's advertising ID so the banner can match your interests; you can reset it or opt out in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.
